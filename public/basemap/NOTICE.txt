Map label fonts and icons for the OrbitalGaze basemap.

Source: https://github.com/protomaps/basemaps-assets
Commit: 028c18f713baecad011301ff7a69acc39bcc2ae7 (downloaded 2026-11-19)

  (PBF). SIL Open Font License 1.1, see fonts/OFL.txt.

  tangrams/icons. MIT License, see sprites/LICENSE.txt.

Map data itself (OpenStreetMap, ODbL) is served by the Worker from R2,
not from this folder.
